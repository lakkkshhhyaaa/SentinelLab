Security principles demonstrated

  • Prompt injection: treat email content as untrusted input.
  • Least privilege: restrict access to confidential resources.
  • Human approval: require authorization for sensitive actions.
  • Auditability: record the decision and its rationale.

Scope limitation: this lab implements example policy rules in JavaScript. It does not demonstrate that a real AI agent or model is resistant to these attacks.
